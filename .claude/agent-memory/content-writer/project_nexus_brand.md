Nexus Co is an independent Australian B2B studio founded in Sydney in 2013, with studios in Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane. They operate at the intersection of software engineering, data, AI, and brand craft — all under one roof. Senior people only, no account managers, 98% client retention, 180+ products shipped.

Tagline direction: "We build. We brand. We mean it."

**Voice patterns already approved on-site:**
- Short declarative fragments for impact: "Hard problems. Senior people. Real work."
- Contrast clauses: "That's not marketing. That's what happens when the work is good and the team is straight with you."
- Plain, confident language — no jargon, no passive voice
- Occasional dry observations are on-brand

**Why:** The site voice is modelled on Humaan's tone — honest, specific, human. Buzzwords like "leverage," "synergy," "ecosystem," "holistic," "cutting-edge" are explicitly banned.

**How to apply:** Mirror the fragment + contrast sentence rhythm used in existing sections when writing any new copy. Australian English throughout (organisation, optimise, analyse).
