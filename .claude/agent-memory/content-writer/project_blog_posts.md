**Content patterns established:**
- Content field: plain text, paragraphs separated by `\n\n`, 4–5 paragraphs, 350–600 words (the fintech case study `fintech-data-pipeline-rebuild` was expanded to 9 paragraphs / ~835 words with readingTime: 8 as a richer case study format — this is an exception, not the new default)
- Voice: senior practitioner, no corporate filler, dry observations welcome, Australian English
- Case Studies: open with the client's real problem (often a mistaken brief), then show the discovery process, then the outcome. Outcomes are specific — numbers, timelines, a named decision that changed.
- Engineering: acknowledge the demo/tutorial version upfront, then show where production diverges. End with a plain honest summary of when the approach does and doesn't make sense.
- Design & Brand: lead with the common mistake, then the counter-argument. Brand strategy posts are always anchored in product decisions, not aesthetics.
- Process & Insights: define what makes the process fail, then show the working version. End with a transferable principle, not a sales pitch.

**How to apply:** When adding new blog posts, use the same `content` paragraph format, draw from the existing author list, and match the content pattern for the relevant category. See [[project-nexus-brand]] for voice rules.
