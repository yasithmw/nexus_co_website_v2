The Philosophy section (Section 3 of the About page) sits on a dark background (#1a1057) and originally had three large statement paragraphs. The redesign reduces it to a section label, one punchy headline, and two or three stacked single-sentence statements.

**Emphasis:** Human-first approach — the people, the relationships, the craft. Anchored in fundamentals: thinking, getting the brief right before opening any tool.

**Why:** The old version was too long and heavy for the visual treatment. The dark section with large display type needs fewer, sharper words to land the weight.

**How to apply:** When updating or iterating on this section, keep to the short-statement format. Do not reintroduce paragraph-length copy here. See [[project-nexus-brand]] for voice rules.
